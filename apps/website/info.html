Limits and retention for the xmp.pm public XMPP service.

Home Legal

Hosting and encryption

  • The server runs in Oracle Cloud’s Switzerland region, where service data is normally stored.
  • Oracle is a U.S.-based infrastructure provider and may be required to respond to lawful government requests.
  • OMEMO end-to-end encryption is designed to keep encrypted message content unreadable to the server.
  • Connection and communication metadata is not protected by OMEMO and may be processed and retained.

Retention

  • MAM chat archive: 7 days by default, with opt-out if your client supports it.
  • Offline messages: 30 days or until delivered, whichever is sooner.
  • HTTP uploads: 7 days.
  • Operational, security, and abuse logs: up to 7 days.
  • Encrypted backups: 14 daily snapshots.
  • Invite tokens: 14 days or one use.
  • Deleted accounts: removed live immediately; backups age out within 14 days.
  • Website analytics/tracking: none run by xmp.pm; no analytics script, tracking pixel, or analytics cookie is added.
  • Web edge provider: Cloudflare serves the website and invite pages and may process HTTP metadata for delivery, security, rate limiting, and abuse prevention.

Limits

  • HTTP upload quota: 100 MiB per user.
  • Maximum HTTP upload size: 100 MiB per file.
  • Concurrent sessions: up to 10 per user.
  • Multi-user chat is available, but abusive rooms may be removed.

Service metadata

  • Organization: private person.
  • Bus factor: 1.
  • Free of charge: true; donations are optional.
  • Server region: Oracle Cloud Switzerland.
  • Since: 2026-06-14.

Testing permission

Automated XMPP provider checks, TLS checks, uptime checks, and compliance checks are allowed. Abuse, probing, and pentesting remain forbidden without explicit approval.
